Northfield Institute Scholarship FAQ

Merit scholarship: Students ranked in the top 5 percent of their branch in the previous
academic year receive a 25 percent tuition waiver. No separate application is needed;
the scholarship cell publishes the list by 15 August.

Need-based aid: Families with an annual income below 4 lakh rupees may apply for aid of
up to 40 percent of tuition. The application requires an income certificate, the latest
tax return and a statement of purpose. Applications open on 1 July and close on 31 July.

Document verification: All supporting documents must be uploaded to the student portal in
PDF format. Originals are checked at the scholarship cell within ten days of the
application deadline. Incomplete applications are held for one week and then rejected.

Disbursement: Approved aid is adjusted against the next semester fee. It is not paid in
cash. Students on disciplinary probation lose eligibility for that academic year.

Appeals: A rejected applicant may appeal within seven days by writing to the Dean of
Student Affairs, with evidence addressing the specific rejection reason.
