Northfield Institute Library Policy (2026)

Borrowing: Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for 14 days.
Reserved titles marked with a red sticker may be kept for 7 days only. A reserved book
that is returned late attracts a fee of 5 rupees per day per book, capped at 200 rupees
per item.

Renewals: Each book can be renewed twice online if nobody has placed a hold. Renewal
must be done on or before the due date; a book already overdue cannot be renewed.

Waivers: The Chief Librarian may waive late fees for medical reasons when a certificate
is submitted within 10 days of the due date. Fee waivers for official college events
must be requested by the faculty coordinator in writing.

Lost books: A lost book must be replaced with the same edition or the current market
price plus a 100 rupee processing charge. Theft must be reported to campus security and
the library within 24 hours.
