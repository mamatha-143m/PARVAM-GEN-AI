Northfield Institute Hostel FAQ

Check-in and check-out: The hostel opens for first-year check-in on 20 July. Rooms are
allotted by the warden's office after fee payment. A student leaving the hostel must
complete check-out at least 48 hours before vacating and return the room key to the
caretaker.

Gate timings: The main gate closes at 22:00 on weekdays and 23:00 on Saturdays. Late
entry requires written approval from the warden and is recorded in the gate register.
Repeated late entries lead to a parent-teacher meeting.

Visitors: Visitors are allowed in the common room between 10:00 and 18:00 and must sign
the visitor register. Overnight visitors are not permitted. Parents may visit rooms with
the warden's permission.

Food: The mess serves breakfast from 07:30 to 09:00, lunch from 12:30 to 14:00 and
dinner from 19:30 to 21:00. Special diet requests must be submitted to the mess
committee at the start of each month.

Maintenance: Report electrical or plumbing issues through the hostel app. Urgent issues
such as water leakage are attended to within four hours; other complaints within two
working days.
